01 HAIR COVER

Hair Restoration

NIVA covers eligible hair restoration treatments, so the cost of a transplant or related care doesn’t fall on you alone. Once your claim is approved, your policy reimburses the eligible share.

Reimbursement70%
Annual limit$6,000
Claim decision~5 days

02 SKIN & LASER COVER

Laser & Skin Treatments

NIVA covers eligible laser and skin treatments with a documented medical need, such as scarring or pigmentation. Each approved session is reimbursed up to your annual limit.

Reimbursement60%
Annual limit$3,500
Claim decision~5 days

How a claim works

  1. 1Check your session limits
  2. 2Get pre-authorisation
  3. 3Attend sessions with your provider
  4. 4Submit each claim & get reimbursed
Your policy coverage
Coverage
60% up to $3,500 / yr
Waiting period
6 months
Pre-authorization
Required beyond 3 sessions
Co-pay
15%

Not covered: Purely aesthetic skin-tone treatments without a documented medical indication.

03 FACIAL SURGERY COVER

Facial Surgery

NIVA covers eligible facial surgery when it is medically or reconstructively supported, so you can plan the cost with confidence. We reimburse the approved share — we never perform or recommend procedures.

Reimbursement50%
Annual limit$9,000
Claim decision~7 days
