Here is the exact checklist of documents, data points, and digital assets your platform must collect from the user during onboarding for Hair Transplants, Rhinoplasty, Tummy Tucks, and Liposuction.

1. General Prerequisites (Required for ALL 4 Surgeries)

These foundational documents establish the identity of the parties, the exact scope of the insured event, and a clean baseline of the user's health.
• Official Clinic Booking Confirmation & Invoice: A PDF copy of the surgical reservation showing the scheduled date, the clinic's tax registration number, and the exact names of the procedures being performed.
• Government-Issued Photo ID: To verify age requirements (e.g., locking out minors from rhinoplasty/hair transplants) and cross-reference the name on the medical files.
• Pre-Operative Medical Clearance Report: A signed form from a general physician or the operating surgeon confirming the patient is fit for surgery, listing their active height, weight (to auto-calculate BMI), and blood pressure.
• Comprehensive Laboratory Blood Panel: A PDF copy of blood tests taken within 30 days of the surgery date, specifically checking:
	• Complete Blood Count (CBC): To screen for pre-existing anemia or low platelets.
	• Coagulation Profile (PT/INR/APTT): To rule out pre-existing bleeding disorders that could cause massive hematomas.
• Signed Medical History Disclosure Form: A digital questionnaire inside your app where the user legally certifies their smoking status, history of blood clots (DVT), and diabetic status.

2. Surgery-Specific Document & Detail Prerequisites

In addition to the general documents, the user must upload specific clinical assets corresponding to their selected procedure to establish an indisputable "Day 0" baseline.

Hair Transplant

• Pre-Op Trichoscopy / Hair Density Report: A copy of the surgeon’s initial assessment sheet stating the calculated donor density score (follicular units per cm²) and the targeted number of grafts to be harvested.
• 5-Angle Baseline Photo Dossier: High-resolution photographs captured directly through the app’s camera module (preventing uploads of old or edited photos) showing:
	1. Frontal hairline (head tilted down)
	2. Top/Vertex crown view
	3. Left profile temporal view
	4. Right profile temporal view
	5. Back of the scalp (the donor zone)

Rhinoplasty

• Pre-Op Acoustic Rhinometry / Airflow Test (If available): A diagnostic baseline printout measuring initial nasal airway breathing capacity. If the surgeon did not perform one, the app must require a signed statement from the surgeon certifying the airway is currently clear.
• Standardized Rhinoplasty Photographic Baseline: High-resolution photos taken using the app’s guided framing overlay:
	1. Direct frontal view
	2. Left lateral profile (exact 90 degrees)
	3. Right lateral profile (exact 90 degrees)
	4. Basal view (looking up the nostrils to document pre-existing septal deviation)
	5. Oblique views (left and right three-quarter angles)

Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck)

• Pre-Op Abdominal Ultrasound Report: A diagnostic scan report documenting the exact pre-existing width of any diastasis recti (muscle separation). This is crucial so the patient cannot claim a pre-existing muscle gap as a post-op failure.
• Cotinine Lab Test Results: For high-risk procedures like tummy tucks, a lab report confirming zero nicotine in the system within 14 days of the operation date.
• Belly/Torso Baseline Photo Dossier: App-guided photos taken at standing rest:
	1. Direct front view of the abdomen
	2. Left lateral view
	3. Right lateral view
	4. Flexed/Bent forward view (to document the exact volume of pre-op loose skin hanging)

Liposuction

• Pre-Op Target Zone Volume Metrics: A copy of the surgeon's consultation notes defining the exact anatomical regions targeted for fat extraction (e.g., "bilateral flanks and outer thighs") and the estimated fat extraction volume.
• Skin Elasticity / Recoil Clinical Note: A signed confirmation from the doctor stating the patient possesses adequate skin snap-back quality.
• Target Zone Photo Dossier: Close-up, high-resolution photos of the specific body parts scheduled for liposuction, taken from the front, back, and side profiles, highlighting skin texture to prevent pre-existing stretch marks or cellulite from being blamed as a surgical failure later.
